# Credits

The template ships no image or sound files of its own: its sprites are painted in code and its sounds come from the game layer's placeholder pack, which the binary embeds.

| What | Where | Author | License |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sprites: the ship, enemies, boss, bullets, sparks and items | `art.go`, painted at startup | NSGo Game Contributors | Apache-2.0 |
| Sounds: `shoot`, `hit`, `explosion`, `powerup`, `select` and the `music_loop` music | `github.com/nsgo-dev/game/pkg/placeholder`, loaded with `placeholder.Sound` | NSGo Game Contributors | [CC0-1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) |

When you replace them with files of your own, list each file here with its license.
