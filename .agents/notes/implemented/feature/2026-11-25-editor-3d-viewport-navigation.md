# Agent Note: The editor's 3D viewport navigates like a mature editor and remembers its camera per scene

Status: implemented

## Problem

The 3D viewport could orbit with a drag and dolly with the wheel, nothing more. A touchpad scroll zoomed instead of panning, `BeginViewport3DDrag`'s pan was never reached, F did nothing in 3D (the viewport's own `f` binding, newer than the command's, only framed in 2D), there was no axis view, no orthographic projection and no way to fly through a level, and every scene opened at the default camera (#891).

## Decision

- **One camera, two projections** (`editor/viewport3d.go`): `OrbitCamera3D` gains `Orthographic`. The orthographic view is `OrthoHeight` high, `2 * Distance * tan(FOV/2)`, the height the perspective view shows at Target, so switching keeps what lies at Target the same size and `Distance` still zooms. In orthographic the eye moves back along the view to at least half the clip range (`eyeDistance64`) so the near plane does not cut the scene in front of Target. `Project`, `projectSegment`, `ScreenRay` and `SceneCamera` share `toCamera64` and `screen64`; an orthographic ray starts on the eye's plane under the pixel and runs along the view, so `Pick3D`, `pickGeometry3D` and every gizmo drag work unchanged. `GizmoLength3D` sizes the gizmo by `Distance` in orthographic, where the distance from the eye no longer changes its size on screen.
- **A basis that holds looking straight down**: `basis64` derives right from Yaw alone (`(cos yaw, 0, -sin yaw)`) and up from right and forward, instead of crossing forward with the world's up, which is degenerate at a pitch of ±90°. The axis views (`View3D`, `OrbitCamera3D.SetView`) therefore look exactly along their axes, Top with -Z at the top of the view, and `SceneCamera` looks at Target with the camera's own up. `Orbit` still stops at `maxOrbitPitch`, short of the poles, so a drag never flips the view.
- **Navigation is editor state, never an edit** (`editor/navigate3d.go`): `SetView3D`, `SetOrthographic3D`, `OrbitView3D`, `ResetView3D`, `PanView3D`, `ZoomView3D` and the fly methods change `Camera3D` and notify; none goes through `History`. Panning moves Target by `worldPerPixel`, so what lies at Target follows the pointer exactly in both projections.
- **Flying** (`BeginFly3D`, `FlyLook3D`, `FlyMove3D`, `EndFly3D`, `FlySpeed3D`): the right button starts a flight that turns the camera about its eye (`OrbitCamera3D.Look`) and moves eye and Target together (`Fly`). The pointer is locked to the viewport frame with `Ref.LockPointer` and turned by `OnPointerDelta` where `Env.CanLockPointer`, and by mouse moves with the right button held elsewhere. While flying, a `core.Show` binds W, A, S, D, Q and E (and their Shift forms) as no-ops, which as the newest bindings win over the gizmo's W and S, and runs a `RequestAnimationFrame` loop that reads `core.KeyHeld` and moves the camera by the frame's time, capped at 100 ms. A flight switches an orthographic camera to perspective, because looking around in a parallel projection only slides the view.
- **Gestures** (`editor/viewport3d_ui.go`, `viewport3DGestures`): a middle-button drag and a Shift drag pan through `BeginViewport3DDrag(pan=true)`; a touchpad scroll without Control or Command pans, the wheel and a scroll with them zoom, a pinch zooms by its scale ratio; two fingers on a touch screen cancel a one-finger gizmo drag or orbit and pan by their center. The viewport's `f` binding now frames in 3D too, and frame selection fits the bounds' sphere into the orthographic height across the narrower side of the view.
- **Shortcuts and UI**: commands `view.3d.front` (1), `view.3d.back` (ctrl+1), `view.3d.right` (3), `view.3d.left` (ctrl+3), `view.3d.top` (7), `view.3d.bottom` (ctrl+7) and `view.3d.orthographic` (5), Blender's layout on the digit row, run only in 3D. Over the scene, as in the `Viewport3D.dc.html` artboard: a bar with Persp and Ortho, the view's name (`ViewName3D`), Focus and Reset view; an axis widget, a canvas whose six axis ends turn to the matching view, the end nearer the viewer winning where two overlap, with Orbit 15° buttons; and the controls' help line. The status bar reads "<view> · yaw N° · pitch N°". The camera snaps; nothing animates.
- **Per-scene view state** (`editor/viewstate.go`): `.game/editor.yaml` (`ViewStateFile`) lists each scene's `camera3D` by its project path, version 1, unknown fields, duplicate paths, paths outside the project and non-finite numbers refused, sorted by path so a save is deterministic; it runs `formattest.Run` and has its entry on the File Formats pages. The editor saves the open scene's view as another scene replaces it (`openYAMLWithLayers`), on `Save` and `SaveFile`, as the window closes and on a quit request, and restores it after `OpenFile` and after `OpenProject` opens its main scene (the main scene opens before the new project is current, so the lookup waits until it is). A scene without a view opens at `DefaultOrbitCamera3D`. A save drops scenes whose files are gone and writes nothing when the bytes are unchanged; a damaged file restores nothing and is rewritten by the next save. Creating `.game`, the editor writes `.game/.gitignore` with `*`, so the views stay out of version control even in a project whose `.gitignore` does not list the folder.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep the view in the scene file**, as some engines keep an editor camera in the scene: one file, nothing to ignore. Rejected because moving the camera would dirty the scene and every review diff, which the issue and the repository's text-file rules exist to prevent.
- **Keep the view in `game.yaml`'s editor section**: the project schema already has `editor`. Rejected because it is shared, reviewed configuration; a per-person camera there would churn on every session.
- **A separate `OrthoHeight` field on the camera**: an orthographic zoom independent of Distance. Rejected because switching projections would then change what the view shows at Target, and every zoom, pan and focus path would need a second case; deriving it from Distance keeps one zoom.
- **Fly by key repeat (`KeyOptions.Repeat`)** instead of a frame loop: no per-frame callback. Rejected because repeat timing depends on the platform's settings and stutters at its start, and nsgotest's `Advance` drives frames deterministically.
- **Keypad keys for the axis views**, Blender's default: `OnKey` binds by key name, which a keypad digit shares with the digit row (only `KeyEvent.Code`, such as `Numpad1`, tells them apart), so the keypad cannot be bound on its own; the digit row with Ctrl for the opposite view is bound instead.
- **Writing `.game/` into every project template's `.gitignore`**: what a project should do anyway. Rejected as the only guard because existing projects would keep committing the views; the folder's own `.gitignore` covers them, and templates being reworked in parallel are left alone.

## Consequences

- Benefits: the 3D viewport navigates with a mouse, a touchpad, a touch screen and the keyboard; orthographic plans and elevations pick and drag correctly; each scene reopens where it was left without touching a reviewed file.
- Costs: `basis64` no longer follows the eye-to-target vector, so code that set Target and Eye independently would not see its own forward; nothing does. A flight drops orthographic. The axis widget's ends are drawn on a canvas, so a keyboard user reaches the axis views through the shortcuts and the command palette, not the widget. The editor writes `.game/editor.yaml` and `.game/.gitignore` into any project it opens a scene of.
