# Agent Note: Kinematic Bodies, Sensors, Tiles, Queries and Contact Events for 2D Rigid Bodies

Status: implemented

## Problem

`RigidWorld` and the kinematic layer (`World`, `Body`, `Trigger`, `TileGrid`) did not connect (#805). A `RigidBody` could not be raycast or overlap-queried, fell through a tilemap level, could not be an animated platform that pushes crates, and reported no contacts, so a game could not play impact sounds or deal damage. `physics3d.World` already has kinematic bodies, triggers and contact events; Godot's `AnimatableBody2D`, `body_entered` and `PhysicsDirectSpaceState2D` and Box2D's contact and sensor events are the references.

## Decision

- **Kinematic bodies (`rigid.go`, `rigid_kinematic.go`)**: `RigidBody.Kinematic` is a flag beside `Static`, not a body-type enum, so existing literals keep working; `Static` wins when both are set, which keeps segments and chains static. `fixed()` (static or kinematic) replaces `Static` wherever the solver means "infinite mass": broadphase pairs, island links, the positional pass, `applyImpulseRaw` and the `Apply*` methods. `awakeDynamic()` excludes kinematic bodies, so a kinematic body is never in an island, never sleeps and never meets a static body or tiles. `prepareMass` zeroes `InvMass` and `InvInertia` of a kinematic body and leaves `Mass` and `Inertia`, which derive them again when the flag is cleared, so joints (which scale by `InvMass`) treat it as an anchor without changes. Velocity integration skips it; position integration moves it. `SetTargetPose` is Box2D's `b2Body_SetTargetTransform` without the time step argument: the step's own `h` gives the velocity, so carried bodies see the platform's motion, and the step then snaps the pose to the target exactly, because integrating `(target - pose) / h * h` in float32 can miss by an ulp and an animation must land where it says. A body given no new target stops at the start of the next step, unless its velocity is no longer the one the target gave (the game set another), which matches `AnimatableBody2D`: a platform the animation does not move does not move. A moving kinematic body wakes the sleepers it touches (`wakeTouched`, cached contacts in `wakeDisturbed`) and keeps the dynamic bodies it touches from accumulating rest time.
- **Sensors (`rigid_events.go`)**: `RigidBody.Sensor` works on any kind of body. The broadphase keeps a pair when exactly one body is a sensor, whatever the kinds, drops a pair of sensors, and drops a pair of other bodies when both are fixed, as it dropped two static bodies before (`rigidPairCandidate`); contact detection, waking and the positional pass skip any pair with a sensor, and continuous collision neither sweeps sensors nor stops at them. After the step's last pose change (after shapes are synced), one more broadphase pass runs only when a sensor exists, and a pair with a manifold from `collideRigidPair` is an overlap. A sensor never detects a sensor (Box2D v3). `World.MoveAndSlide` skips static sensors in `World.RigidWorld`.
- **Tiles (`rigid_tiles.go`)**: `RigidWorld.Tiles` is the existing `TileGrid` interface, so `tilemap` layers and `Grid` plug in as they do for `World`. A shared private static body (`tileBody`) stands for the tiles in manifolds, the warm-start cache, continuous collision and `inWorld`, with `sub` = `row*cols + col`, so the solver, warm starting, sleeping and the cache need no tile cases. Its friction, restitution and layers come from `TileFriction` (zero means `DefaultTileFriction` = 0.4, the default of a body, a negative value frictionless), `TileRestitution`, `TileLayer` and `TileMask` each step. Each awake dynamic body is collided against the solid and one-way cells its hull's bounds meet, in row-major order, as `cellHull` boxes whose shared edges are computed from `(col+1)*tw` so neighbours meet exactly. `tileFace` removes seam snags: a manifold whose normal leans into a face a solid neighbour covers (beyond `tileNormalTolerance` = 1e-3) is clipped again against the open face nearest its normal, as `chainSegmentManifold` does at a flat vertex, and dropped when no open face faces the body. A point an earlier tile already reports with the same normal is dropped (`withoutReportedPoints`). A one-way cell only has its top face and only catches a body whose deepest point is within `max(0, VY*h) + oneWayTileMargin` (1 px) of the top, or that the cache shows it held on the last step, so a body resting on it never falls through and one from below passes. The positional pass corrects a body once per surface direction by the deepest of the tiles sharing the normal; correcting once per tile pushed a box standing on three tiles 1.5 times its depth, off the floor, so the next step found no contact. `appendTilePieces` adds solid cells with an open face, and one-way cells a body starts above and moves down into, to the sweep's candidates, so the existing supports and `behindSupport` logic stop fast bodies on tiles without catching on seams. When the grid's geometry (`tileGeomOf`: size, origin, cell size) changes, the cache's tile entries no longer name the same cells: `prepareTiles` drops them and wakes their bodies; a sleeping body also wakes when its cell stops being solid or one-way (`tileContactChanged`).
- **Queries (`rigid_query.go`, `world.go`)**: `RigidWorld.Raycast`, `RaycastFirst`, `QueryPoint` and `QueryOverlap` reuse `World`'s hit types, `QueryMask`, `raycastShape`, `raycastTiles` and the tile overlap loop (moved into `appendTileOverlaps`), with a `RigidBody` field on `RaycastHit` and `OverlapHit` as `ShapeCastHit` already had. Bodies are tested as `RigidBody.WorldShape`: a box with a non-zero `Angle` becomes a polygon, because the stored `Shape.Box` stays axis-aligned. `QuerySensors` is a new bit outside `QueryAll`, so sensors are skipped by default, as Godot's `collide_with_areas` and Box2D v3's queries do. Order is the `World` contract: rays stable-sorted by `T` with bodies before tiles, overlaps bodies in `Bodies` order then tiles row-major.
- **Contact events**: `updateContactEvents` runs right after detection, before warm starting, so `ApproachSpeed` is the pre-solve closing speed (`prepareManifold` records the fastest point's `-vn` per manifold). A touch is a pair `(a, b)` with at least one manifold; every manifold the narrowphase returns has a point that overlaps, so speculative points alone never begin a contact. Manifolds of one pair are consecutive (chain segments, and all tiles of one body, which count as one contact so a crate sliding over a tiled floor does not begin a contact per tile). The begin event takes the manifold approaching fastest and its deepest point. A previous touch not found this step carries over (`touchLasts`) only while the pair can still be tested later: both bodies are in the world, neither is a sensor, `rigidPairCandidate` and `CanCollideRigid` hold, no joint keeps them apart, and a body touching tiles is dynamic. Then it lasts when neither body is awake and dynamic, so sleeping piles stay silent, or while the two are within `contactEndGap` (0.25 px, a little more than the gap continuous collision leaves); otherwise it ends. Requiring a testable pair fixes a contact that never ended once a resting crate was made kinematic or static, since nothing would test that pair again; the gap is a hysteresis that lets a contact begun by continuous collision at a 0.1 px gap last until the regular contacts find the overlap, and it delays the end of a separating pair by at most one step. Begin events follow detection order, end events the previous touch list's order, and then come the begins continuous collision adds. Lookups go through scratch maps that are only looked up, never iterated.
- **Continuous collision impacts (`rigid_ccd.go`)**: a fast body stopped by the sweep used to get one impulse at the one point the sweep reached first. A box landing flat on a floor took that impulse at a corner, so it kept most of its fall speed and gained a spin; on the next steps the sweep stopped it at 0.1 px again with no turn (the sweep restarts at `t = 0`), its speed grew every step and it never began a contact or slept, on a static floor (on `main` too) as on tiles. Across a tile seam the corner was one tile's, and the box tumbled with several begin/end pairs. `resolveImpact` now collects the points where the body's face meets every candidate piece within `ccdImpactGap` of it whose normal matches the impact's (by moving the body's hull a little along the normal and taking `collideHullsWithin`'s manifold, deduplicated across tiles by `withoutReportedPoints`), and runs 16 sequential-impulse passes over them with the same restitution rule, so a flat landing loses its normal velocity at both corners without turning. Since the impact removes the approach speed before any later manifold sees the contact, `beginImpact` emits the begin event from the impact itself, with the fastest point's pre-impulse speed, and adds the touch, unless the pair already touches; the next steps' detection then matches it. Box2D v3 solves the same problem with speculative contacts; they are rejected here because manifolds would change for every world and every golden trajectory.
- **Shape casts**: `RigidWorld.ShapeCast*` now sweep against `RigidWorld.Tiles` with `sweepTilesFirst` / `sweepTilesAll`, as `World`'s do, and select bodies with `queryMatches`, so `QuerySensors` alone selects only sensors as in the other queries.
- **Snapshots (`snapshot.go`)**: body flags and target state, a `*Grid` `Tiles` (via `snapshotGrid` / `restoreGrid`, shared with `World`, now with the grid's origin), tile settings, tile cache entries (`Tile`, `BodyBIndex` -1, `Sub`), and the touch and sensor lists are saved; entries with bodies outside `Bodies` are dropped, as cache entries already were. `Restore` recomputes `tileGeom` from the restored grid so the next step does not see a geometry change, and clears the event lists. `SnapshotVersion` goes from 1 to 2 and `Restore` reads both: the new fields change what a body is (a kinematic platform restored by a version 1 reader would be dynamic, and a tile cache entry with `BodyBIndex` -1 would fail as out of range), so a reader that only knows version 1 must refuse these snapshots instead of letting `encoding/json` drop the fields. A version 1 snapshot has none of them and restores as before. `Checksum` hashes the new body state only when set and the tile and event state only when used. The touch list is state the next step depends on, so a world whose bodies touch now hashes it: `TestRollbackPhysics2DSoak`'s golden changes (to `0x62830a39310934bd`, the same on amd64 and arm64) while its trajectory does not, which hashing without the new section confirmed; `TestGoldenTrajectory2D` keeps its hash.

## Alternatives considered

### Merge solid tiles into chain loops or rectangles

Tracing the outline of the solid regions into `Chain` loops would reuse chain ghost suppression wholesale, and greedy rectangle merging would leave no seams on flat runs. Both are rejected because they need a rebuild, cached per grid, whenever a tile changes, and the `TileGrid` interface has no change notification; `tilemap` edits a level at runtime. Per-cell boxes with the open-face rule read the grid as it is each step, cost only the cells under each body, and give the same seam behaviour on flat runs.

### One query surface on `World` that also sees `World.RigidWorld`

The issue allows either. Extending `World.Raycast` and friends to rigid bodies would change what existing `World` queries return for games that already set `World.RigidWorld` for `MoveAndSlide`. Methods on `RigidWorld` with the same hit types leave `World` unchanged and still share the types and helpers.

### Events per tile, or per manifold

Box2D reports one begin per shape pair and treats a chain as one shape; Godot reports `body_entered` once per TileMap. Per-tile events would make a sliding crate play an impact sound at every seam, so all tiles of a body are one contact and the event names the tile its point is on.

### Contacts that begin on speculative points

Box2D v3 counts a manifold with only speculative points as touching. Here such points exist only inside manifolds of overlapping shapes, so the question does not arise, and beginning on actual overlap means a body passing within 1 px of another reports nothing.

### `RigidWorld` queries with `World`'s signatures

`World.Raycast(from, to, mask)` leaves the layer mask to `RaycastLayer`, and `World.QueryPoint(x, y)` takes no mask at all. Copying that split onto `RigidWorld` would need a third variant of `QueryPoint` to reach sensors, so `RigidWorld`'s queries take `(mask, layerMask)` directly, zero meaning all; the docs say code moved between the two adds or drops those arguments. `ContactEvent` keeps `ContactBegin` and `ContactEnd` and the normal from `BodyA` toward `BodyB`, the convention of the 2D `Contact` from `TestShapes`; `physics3d` reports enter, stay and exit with the normal from `BodyB` toward `BodyA`, and its doc and the 2D one point at each other.

### Kinematic bodies as a third value of a body-type field

An enum (`Static`, `Kinematic`, `Dynamic`) is clearer. It is rejected because `Static` is a public field used by every constructor, snapshot and game; a second flag keeps them all working.

## Consequences

- **Benefits**: a crate dropped on a tile floor rests and sleeps on it, boxes and capsules slide and circles roll across tile seams at full speed, a one-way platform holds from above and lets a body jump through from below, a 3000 to 12000 px/s shot stops on a one-tile floor and reports its impact speed, a box dropped 900 px onto a static floor or tiles reports one begin at about 1040 px/s and sleeps level, a fast box landing across a tile seam stays level with one begin, and a kinematic platform driven by velocity or `SetTargetPose` lands exactly on its targets and carries a crate at its speed while the crate leaves it unmoved. Raycasts hit turned boxes at their true pose with point and normal; a box landing on a floor reports one begin with its impact speed and an end when lifted; a 27-body scene with tiles, a ramp, a moving platform and a sensor gives the same event log across runs and across JSON `Save`/`Restore` at any step. A tile level of 200 awake boxes steps in about 1 ms without allocating (`BenchmarkRigidWorldTileLevel`, one core of a Ryzen 9 3900X on Linux); the 500-body packed benchmark still steps in about 5.2 ms.
- **Costs**: `RigidWorld.Checksum` differs from earlier releases for any world whose bodies touch, since it hashes the touch list. A contact ends once the bodies are 0.25 px apart rather than at the first step without overlap. Tiles push only dynamic bodies, so a kinematic platform passes through walls and sensors do not see tiles. Kinematic bodies are not swept for continuous collision and fast bodies are not swept against them, so a fast kinematic body can pass through a thin dynamic one. A body touching several tile surfaces at once (a floor and a wall) reports one contact. A contact or sensor overlap with a body removed between `Step` and `Save` does not report its end after `Restore`. Every step with tiles rebuilds a lookup of the cache when a one-way tile is involved, and every step with a sensor runs one more broadphase pass.
- **Testing**: `rigid_contact_test.go` covers resting and sleeping on tiles, walls and ceilings, sliding and rolling over seams with no rise, tilt or lost speed, one-way tiles from above and below, fast shots on a one-tile floor, fast landings of boxes and a bullet circle on a static floor and on tiles with their impact speed, one begin and sleep, fast boxes landing across seams staying level, contacts ending when a resting crate becomes kinematic or static, shape casts against tiles and sensors only, version 1 snapshots restoring, waking when a tile is removed, tile layers, the kinematic platform in both drive modes, pushing without being pushed, waking a crate when a platform starts, raycasts against a turned box with layers, sensors and tiles, point and overlap queries, landing begin/end events on a body floor and a tile floor, an end when a body leaves the world, sensor events with sensors not seeing sensors, determinism of a full event log across runs and mid-run JSON restores, and a snapshot round trip with a pending target. `ExampleRigidWorld_tiles` and `ExampleRigidBody_kinematic` show the API.

See [sleeping, broadphase, warm starting and CCD](2026-11-25-physics-rigid-sleep-broadphase-warm-start-ccd.md) for the solver this builds on and [snapshots and determinism](2026-11-25-physics-save-determinism.md) for the snapshot contract.
