Where. Bottom center of the viewport, 8 px above its bar; never over the tree, inspector or agent panel. With no viewport on screen (Start, Settings), bottom center of the window, 24 px up.
One at a time. Others wait in order and the visible toast shows how many with “+2”. A repeat of the same event merges into the one waiting; nothing jumps ahead of an error or a running export.
How long. Success and info 4 s, 6 s with an action; warning 8 s; error and progress stay. Hover, keyboard focus or the window going to the background pauses the timer.
Motion. 180 ms in, fading and rising 8 px; 120 ms out. With reduced motion it appears and leaves at once.
Keyboard and screen readers. The container is role status, aria-live polite. Focus never jumps to a toast; F6 reaches it, Tab moves between its buttons, Esc dismisses it.
Not for decisions. Permission asks and questions are cards in the agent panel; check failures go to Problems. The event-to-channel table is on the Spec board.
