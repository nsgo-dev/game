### Added

- `game/sceneformat`: added scene version 4 (`scene.v4.json`) with 3D light objects (`light`: directional, point, and spot with color, intensity, range, cone angles, and shadows), scene `environment` (`sky` color/gradient/HDR, ambient and IBL intensity, distance and height `fog`, `tonemapping`, and `exposure`), and 3D camera objects (`camera`: perspective or orthographic, FOV/orthoHeight, near, far, and `current`), with `refs` HDR path scanning/rewriting and `game check` validation (#895).
- `game/scenemodel`: wired scene lights, directional/spot/point shadows, sky cubemaps, IBL, fog, tonemapping, exposure, and active 3D cameras into `Build` and `Renderer` (`PrepareScene`, `RecordShadows`, `DrawSky`, `ClearColor`, `ActiveCamera`) (#895).
- `game/editor`: rendered scene lights, shadows, sky, fog, tonemapping, and exposure in the 3D GPU viewport with parity against `scenemodel`/`render3d`, and added light/camera icons, undoable interactive handles (light direction, range sphere, spot inner/outer cone, camera frustum FOV/far), the Scene Environment settings panel, Light/Camera 3D inspector sections, and the Look Through Scene Camera toggle (`0`) (#895).
