### Added

- `game/render3d`: HDR rendering and tone mapping. `HDRTarget` is an offscreen `gpu.FormatRGBA16Float` target (falling back to `RGBA8Unorm` where half floats are unsupported), and `ToneMapPass` resolves it to the display with exposure (`ExposureFromEV`), a tone-mapping curve (`ToneMapNone`, `ToneMapReinhard`, `ToneMapACES`, `ToneMapNeutral`, `ToneMapAgX`) and exact sRGB or power-law gamma encoding, with `ApplyToneMap` as its CPU reference. `LitPBRPipelineOptions.LinearOutput` (through the new `NewLitPBRPipelineWithOptions`) and `MaterialPipelineOptions.LinearOutput` make the lit PBR and material shaders write linear HDR radiance for it, and `ToneMapPass.DrawDynamicResolution` tone-maps a `DynamicResolutionTarget`'s scaled viewport. `SRGBToLinearChannel` and `SRGBToLinear` invert `LinearToSRGBChannel` and `LinearToSRGB` (#250).
