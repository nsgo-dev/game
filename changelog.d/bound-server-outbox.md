### Security

- `game/pkg/online/netplay`: `WebSocketServer.Send` and `WebTransportServer.Send` no longer write to a client's connection on the caller's goroutine, so a client that stops reading can no longer stall the match's tick, and every other client, for the 10-second write timeout. Each client now has a bounded outbox (`OutboxLimits`, 256 frames and 4 MiB by default) written by its own goroutine: past it the client's oldest unreliable frames are dropped, and a reliable frame that does not fit ends the client's session with `PeerLeft` and the reason `"outbox full"`. A write that fails or times out now closes the connection instead of leaving a partial frame that misframed every later one. `SetOutboxLimits` and `OutboxStats` (`OutboxReporter`) tune and report the outboxes; a game that sends large reliable bursts should raise the limits rather than remove them (GHSA-gw6m-3wq3-4782).
