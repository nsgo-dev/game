### Added

- `game new -template visualnovel` starts a short branching visual novel, *Lantern Crossing*, built on `vn`, `flow`, `save` and `l10n`: two characters with expressions, drawn backgrounds, choices that change the ending, a backlog, auto and skip modes that skip only lines already read, three save slots, and English and Simplified Chinese, played with the keyboard, a controller or touch, with headless tests that reach both endings with each; `nsgo init -template github.com/nsgo-dev/game/template/visualnovel` writes the same project (#801).
