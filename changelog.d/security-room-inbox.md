### Security

- `pkg/online/server`: each room's inbox is now bounded per member (`RoomLimits.PerPeer`, 256 by default), so a match that falls behind its packet loop, or a member that floods it, can no longer grow the server's memory without limit. A full member queue drops that member's oldest unreliable packet, and a reliable packet with nothing to evict is refused with the new `ErrRoomInboxFull`, on which the packet loop should disconnect the peer. `End`, `Leave` and closing a room discard queued packets, `Members` returns peers in join order, the new `Room.Drain` reads members round robin so one sender cannot starve the rest, and `Room.InboxStats` reports the backlog and what the limits dropped or refused. Servers that use `Rooms` should handle `ErrRoomInboxFull` from `Deliver` and may tune the limits with `Rooms.SetLimits` (GHSA-c599-xjg6-jhc4).
