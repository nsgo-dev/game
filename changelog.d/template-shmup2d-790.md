### Added

- `game new -template shmup2d` and `nsgo init -template github.com/nsgo-dev/game/template/shmup2d` create a vertical scrolling shooter: one stage of enemy waves on dive, weave, cross and hover paths, `bullets` patterns, power, bomb and life items dropped by whole waves, a boss with three phases, lives, bombs and a score, with the stage in a YAML format of its own, played with the keyboard, the mouse, a controller or touch, and `nsgotest` playthroughs that reach both the win and the loss (#790).
