### Added

- `game new -template platformer2d` and `nsgo init -template github.com/nsgo-dev/game/template/platformer2d`: a side-scrolling platformer template with a coyote-time and jump-buffered controller, three rooms with camera bounds, spikes, pits, sparks, a checkpoint and a goal, played with the keyboard, a controller or touch, and tests that play it to a win and a loss (#788).
