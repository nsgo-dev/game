### Added

- `template/action3d`: a third-person 3D action adventure template, `game new -template action3d`, with an orbit camera, run, jump and attack animations blended by an `anim3d` state machine, melee enemies, and a key that opens the gate to the goal, played with keyboard and mouse, game controllers and touch, and tested by headless playthroughs to a win and a loss (#794).
