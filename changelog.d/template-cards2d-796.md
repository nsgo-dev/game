### Added

- `game new -template cards2d` and `nsgo init -template github.com/nsgo-dev/game/template/cards2d`: Suit Ladders, a single-player 2D card game template with drag and drop on `game/cards`, a draw pile, a hand and a discard pile, rules enforced in code, the deck and the table in YAML, numbered deals from `game/random`, saves with `game/save`, and nsgotest playthroughs to a win and a loss with the keyboard, a gamepad and touch (#796).
