### Added

- `game/editor`: models go into the 3D viewport from the asset browser. The browser lists the built-in primitives and the project's `.gltf` and `.glb` models, the outputs of `game assets` model targets included, with thumbnails rendered offscreen on the software GPU (`Models`, `ModelThumbnail`); pressing one picks it for the Place tool (`SelectModel`), which in 3D adds an object drawing it where the pointer's ray meets the scene's geometry, or the ground plane at a chosen height, resting on it and optionally snapped to the grid and aligned to the surface (`PlacementAt3D`, `PlaceModelAt3D`, `SetPlaceOptions3D`). Dragging a model onto the viewport places it where it is dropped, and a `.glb` or `.gltf` file dropped from another app is placed when it lies inside the project (`DropModelFiles`). Each placement is one undoable step. The 3D inspector's Model field picks one of the project's models (`ProjectModels`, `SetObjectModel`) (#893).
