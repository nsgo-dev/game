### Added

- `game/physics3d`: `ConvexHull` and `Cylinder` shapes for dynamic bodies, with a deterministic quickhull builder that has a vertex limit (`NewConvexHull`, and `ConvexHullFromMesh` for glTF and `render3d` meshes), GJK and EPA contacts with up to four manifold points against every shape, triangle meshes, heightfields and voxel colliders, raycasts, bounds, mass and inertia from the hull, CCD sweeps, area volumes, debug wireframes, and cover points around standing cylinders (#808). `WorldSnapshot` saves and restores both shapes bit-identically through JSON, and their narrow phase keeps to the cross-architecture FMA-free arithmetic.
