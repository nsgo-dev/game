### Added

- `game/editor`: the 2D viewport drags objects with the Select or Move tool, keeping the offset from where they were grabbed, and draws a transform gizmo on the selection, nested children included: a free-move square, X and Y arrows along the object's own axes, a rotation ring, and corner and edge scale handles about the origin, with Shift keeping the aspect ratio; each drag is one undoable step, Escape or a cancelled gesture puts the object back, drags follow the object's layer under any view or camera zoom and rotation, a scale or rotation that would shear a sprite is refused as `ScaleObject` and `RotateObject` refuse it, and objects hidden or locked in the scene tree are left alone (`BeginDrag2D`, `UpdateDrag2D`, `EndDrag2D`, `CancelDrag2D`, `PickGizmo2D`, `Dragging2D`, `Gizmo2DShown`, `GizmoHandle2D`) (#863).
