### Added

- `game/render3d`: shadows for the first spot light and the first point light of a `LightRig` (#255). `SpotShadowMap` renders a perspective depth map and `PointShadowMap` packs the six cube faces into one 3x2 depth atlas, both updated from the rig's `SpotLight` / `PointLight`, with hard or 3x3 PCF filtering, depth and normal bias, several casters per pass, and `Retained` device-loss recovery. `LitPBRSpotShadowWGSL` / `LitPBRPointShadowWGSL` (`NewLitPBRSpotShadowPipeline`, `NewLitPBRPointShadowPipeline`, or `LitPBRPipelineOptions.SpotShadowLayout` / `PointShadowLayout`) draw lit PBR meshes with the shadow at bind group 3; `SpotShadowWGSL` / `PointShadowWGSL` and their `ForGroup` variants add the shadows to custom shaders, `shade_pbr_visibility` in `LightWGSL` and `ShadePointVisibility` scale the shadowed lights, and `EvaluateSpotShadow` / `EvaluatePointShadow` are the CPU references.
