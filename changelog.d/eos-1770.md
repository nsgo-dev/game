### Added

- `game/pkg/online/eos`: an Epic Online Services boundary, `eos.Platform` over an injected asynchronous `eos.Driver`, with exchange-code login (`eos.ExchangeCodeFromArgs` reads the Epic launcher's `-AUTH_PASSWORD`), achievement unlocks and progress, leaderboard scores and global or friends queries, and rich presence, every result delivered on the engine goroutine; the native EOS SDK adapter is not included yet (#1770).
