### Added

- `game/tilemap`: YAML tilesets (`*.tileset.yaml`, `TilesetDoc`, version 1) define a grid image's or atlas's tiles once, with properties, `solid`/`oneWay`/custom flags, collision shapes, animations and `cardinal` or `blob` terrain sets; `ParseTileset`, `EncodeTileset` and `LoadTilesetFS` read and write them losslessly, `Tilemap.AddTilesetDoc` builds one into a `Tileset` with its flags, `Tileset.Terrains` and `Tilemap.Terrain` hand terrains to `ApplyAutotile`, and the schema is published at `https://game.nsgo.dev/schemas/tileset.v1.json` (#847).
- `game/sceneformat`: scene version 2 tilemaps can be layered grids that place a YAML tileset's tiles (`tilesetFile`) in named `tileLayers`, one readable row per line with `h`, `v` and `d` flip letters, and can name an LDtk level (`level`); `Build` turns them, and Tiled (`.tmj`, `.tmx`) and LDtk (`.ldtk`) sources, into a `tilemap.Tilemap` (`BuiltLayer.Map`) that culls to the scene's view, animates through `BuiltScene.Update` and answers `FlagsAt` and `Raycast`, reading files through the new `FileResolver` and `ImageResolver` interfaces; `FSAssets` reads a project's files so scenes use Tiled and LDtk maps with no resolver of the game's own (#847).
- `game/editor`: the tile tools paint the tile layers of layered grids with flipped tiles (`ActiveTileLayer`, `ActiveTileFlip`), and the tile palette shows a layered grid's YAML tileset with tile layer and flip toggles (#847).
- `game check` validates YAML tilesets, the tilesets and tiles of layered grids, `.tmx` and `.ldtk` tilemap sources and LDtk levels, and `game mv` keeps `tilesetFile` and tileset `image` references working (#847).
