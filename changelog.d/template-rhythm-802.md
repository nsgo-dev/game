### Added

- `game new -template rhythm` and `nsgo init -template github.com/nsgo-dev/game/template/rhythm`: Pulse Lanes, a four-lane rhythm game with its songs and charts in a versioned YAML file over `rhythm` beat maps, a `rhythm.Clock` kept in step with looping music, hits judged by when the key or finger went down, Perfect, Great, Good and Miss judgements, combo, score, rank and an energy bar to clear or fail by, audio and video latency calibration kept per device, saved best clears, and `nsgotest` playthroughs with the keyboard, a game controller, touch and the mouse (#802).
