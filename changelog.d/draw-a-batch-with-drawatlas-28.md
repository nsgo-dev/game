### Changed

- `game/sprite`: `Batch.Draw` draws consecutive sprites that are only moved, rotated and scaled evenly with one NSGo `DrawAtlas` instead of one `DrawImageFrom` each, drawing the same picture (mirrored, unevenly scaled, blended and masked sprites keep their own draw, and `game/scene` layers sorted by Y or a custom key still draw one sprite at a time); recording 5,000 sprites takes 0.59 ms instead of 0.93 ms with 3 allocations instead of 5,000, while the sprite count at 60 fps on a device is not measured yet. A sprite with a NaN or infinite position, origin, rotation, scale or alpha now draws nothing, and a batch with no atlas draws nothing instead of panicking. `game/gametest` adds `ImageDraws` and `AssertSameImages` to check drawn images (with their tint, sampling and clip) however they were recorded, and `RenderOps` renders `DrawAtlas` (#28).
