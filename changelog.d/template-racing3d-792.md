### Added

- `game new -template racing3d` starts a 3D racing game: two laps of a closed circuit against racing-line rivals with `vehicle`'s raycast cars, checkpoints, a countdown, a best-lap ghost saved between launches and a results screen, drawn with `render3d` and played with keyboard, gamepad or touch, with tests that play races to a win and a loss; [Racing 3D template](docs/en/template-racing3d.md) describes it (#792).
